Three things from the news that actually affect your ride — not the politics around them.

Shakti: free bus travel for women

Women with a Karnataka domicile travel free on ordinary BMTC and KSRTC buses inside the state — show a valid Karnataka ID (Aadhaar, voter ID or driving licence). Vajra AC and airport buses are not free. Since 25 November, buses are also meant to carry only as many passengers as there are seats, so expect fuller — but calmer — buses at rush hour.
